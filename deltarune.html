You began to speak to the memory in front of you.

But no matter how much you tried, no words came through.

Suddenly, a voice started speaking in your head.

About how much it longed for you two to be dead.











































But LOVE is stronger than any word.

And so despite the voice's call, you grew bored.

The voice screamed, it begged for you to listen.

It begged to escape its eternal prison.













































But then you closed your mouth.

And in an instant, the meaning of your words was gone.











































"As you were, Vanitas"

"Continue, as you were."
