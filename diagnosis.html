The most important thing for an artist is their colour pallete.

The sketches they draw, however beautiful they may be, are without life.

Colour can bring life or kill a painting.

A sad painting coloured red, a loving one painted blue...

It is the duty of an artist to pick their colours accordingly.

But what if they were undecided?

Is it the best to leave things monochrome, or is it better to colour it wrong?

There is no good anwser to this question, as a truly skilled artist can make anything work.

Am I an artist? Are you an artist?

If you show me your painting, I will show you mine.
